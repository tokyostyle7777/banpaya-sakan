Services

WHAT WE DO

🏠 Interior Plastering

Shikkui, mortar, and diatomite for walls & ceilings. Residential, commercial, and large-scale properties.

🏚 Exterior Plastering

Jolypate, Soton wall, shikkui and mortar finishes for exterior walls. From metal lath and base coat through to the final finish — all in-house.

🔧 Repair & Renovation

Crack repair, resurfacing, and full renovation. Partial repairs to complete overhauls.

🪵 Concrete Floors

Mortar leveling and concrete finishing for garages, parking areas, and large-scale flooring.

🏗 GC / Large-Scale Projects

Specialist plastering subcontractor for general contractors and architects. Scale-flexible.

📋 Other Inquiries

Any plastering-related work is welcome. We'll advise on the full scope of what's possible.
